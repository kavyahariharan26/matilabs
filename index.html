The most valuable data about your agents goes unused.

Real failures from real customers are exactly what it takes to make agents better. Today they just sit in logs.

  • Thousands of long, tangled traces full of retries, duplicates and noise. The few steps that matter are almost impossible to find.

  • Teams can’t easily tell which failures keep repeating, why they happen, or what the right behaviour would have been.

  • Real conversations are full of personal details and private business context, so the lessons never leave the company.

So agents repeat the same mistakes, and the best data for fixing them goes to waste.

Messy logs in. Clean, trusted lessons out.

Logs from any tool go in. Each failure comes out labelled with its root cause and fix, with private details masked. Press run to watch.
